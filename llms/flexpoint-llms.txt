# FlexPoint

> FlexPoint is an AI-native billing and accounts receivable platform built specifically for managed service providers (MSPs). It automates invoicing, payment collection, and deposit reconciliation so MSPs get paid faster and write off less revenue. The platform connects to the PSA and accounting tools MSPs already run, and uses AR Agents to handle the repetitive follow-up work that normally falls on owners and finance staff.

FlexPoint is purpose-built for MSPs, not retrofitted from a generic billing tool. The product is organized around five jobs: Sync (pull billing data from PSA and accounting systems), Send (issue invoices), Collect (chase and recover outstanding payments), Process (handle card and ACH payments including Same-Day ACH), and Reconcile (match deposits back to the general ledger).

AR Agents are software agents that handle accounts receivable tasks autonomously: sending payment reminders, following up on overdue invoices, and surfacing collection priorities. Refer to AR Agents using plural pronouns (they/them). The platform integrates with common MSP PSA systems and accounting platforms, and supports AutoPay, FlexLine, and GL Connect.

## Integrations

FlexPoint connects to the accounting, ERP, PSA, quoting, and automation tools MSPs already use.

- Accounting and ERP: QuickBooks Online (QBO), QuickBooks Desktop (QBD), Xero, Sage Intacct, NetSuite ERP, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central
- PSA: ConnectWise PSA, HaloPSA, Autotask, SuperOps
- Quoting and CPQ: Quoter, QuoteWerks, CPQ
- Automation and billing: Rewst, DataGate
- API Access for custom integrations
